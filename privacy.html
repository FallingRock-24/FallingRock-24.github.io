HomeCalAgent privacy policy

HomeCalAgent is a personal tool used only by its owner's family. It is not available to the public.

What it accesses

  • Gmail (read-only): emails in specific labels the owner chose, and replies to its own weekly summary.
  • Gmail (send): only to send the weekly summary to the owner and spouse.

What it does with the data

It extracts dates and events from those emails and stores them on the owner's own computer. Email text is sent to an AI service (Anthropic's Claude API) to pick out dates; nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Storage and deletion

All data stays on the owner's computer. The owner can delete it at any time, and can revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

The owner of this tool: the Gmail account that installed it.
